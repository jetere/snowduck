SNOWDUCK

Grab coins, climb the castle, spiral up the mountain and snag the Power Stars! Three glowing portal rings near the start lead to sky levels, and a stepping-stone path east leads to Sakura Isle.

WASD / Arrows – run  ·  Mouse or Q/E – camera  ·  X – sprint
Space – jump (chain 3 for a triple jump)
Shift/Z – crouch: + jump while running = long jump, standing = backflip
Shift/Z in the air – ground pound · jump on a wall = wall kick
V – director camera · F – Silent Hill fog · N – map · M – mute · R – respawn · P – settings
Gamepad: stick run, right stick look, A jump, B/X crouch, trigger sprint
